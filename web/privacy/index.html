Privacy Policy

How Confession treats your data with care.

Last updated: August 10, 2026 · Applies to the Confession Discord bot and confess.adore.rest

Overview

Confession (“the bot”, “we”) provides anonymous confession features for Discord servers. Public confession posts do not display Discord usernames or avatars. This policy explains what we store to operate the service safely.

Information we process

  • Discord identifiers — guild IDs, channel IDs, message IDs, and user IDs needed to deliver confessions, replies, reports, and configuration.
  • Confession & reply content — text (and optional image URLs) you submit, stored so posts can be moderated, looked up for abuse, and recovered if needed.
  • Server settings — confession/review channels, word filters, and customization choices set via /setup and /panel.
  • Operational logs — limited join/leave, error, and report events for reliability and safety.

Anonymity

Published confessions are anonymous to other server members. Author identity is retained in private storage for abuse investigation by the bot operator and is not shown on public posts. Owner-only commands such as /lookup exist solely for safety and compliance.

How we use data

  • To post and thread anonymous confessions and replies
  • To power optional staff review and word filtering
  • To process reports and investigate abuse
  • To maintain and improve the bot

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information may be disclosed if required by law, or to Discord when necessary to enforce platform rules. Hosting providers that process infrastructure logs may transiently handle technical metadata.

Retention

Confession records and configuration are kept while needed to operate the bot and handle safety requests. Data for servers that remove Confession may be deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period, unless retention is required for ongoing investigations or legal obligations.

Your choices

  • Do not submit information you do not want stored for moderation purposes.
  • Server admins can remove the bot at any time.
  • Contact us via the support Discord for privacy questions or deletion requests related to your Discord user ID where feasible.

Children

Confession is intended for Discord communities that follow Discord’s age requirements. Do not use the bot if you are not permitted to use Discord.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
