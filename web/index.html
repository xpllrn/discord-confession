Why servers keep Confession

A calm space to share what’s hard to say out loud — with tools mods actually need.

Truly anonymous

Members submit with /confess. Posts never show who wrote them — only the confession itself.

Optional review

Route submissions through a staff channel for approve / reject before they go public.

Replies & reports

Each confession can open a reply thread, and members can report posts that cross the line.

Custom panel

Admins tune avatar, banner, bio, and blocked words from /panel without leaving Discord.

Word filter

Block specific phrases so confessions stay within your community’s rules.

Part of Adore

Same cozy family as Cupid — lavender paper vibes, hosted at confess.adore.rest.
